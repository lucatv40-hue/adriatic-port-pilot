- sea-route.ts: Venice-lagoon marinas follow precomputed channel-centre tracks (src/data/lagoon-tracks.ts, generated from the Laguna Nav engine by tools/lagoon-tracks) because hand-drawn channels cut across land; other lagoon ports exit via inlet channels; sea legs avoid coarse land polygons through offshore gates (tested).
- sea-route.ts routePlan: lagoon departures draw channels to the inlet's safe-water buoy; weather points are equally spaced on the sea leg starting at that buoy (tested).
- map-layers.ts: pure GeoJSON builders for the MapLibre /carta (wind ICON-2I, Copernicus waves+Tp and currents, CAPE classes, notice red/yellow circles); missing grids yield empty layers shown as "non disponibile", never invented (tested). cape.json is read if published; nothing produces it yet.
- route-risk.ts analyses a route only (dense sampling, worst section with entry/exit times); it must never generate an alternative route — the captain chooses.
- route-watch.ts: pure tested worsening rules for saved routes (gust +5 kn, wave +0.4 m, or a user threshold newly crossed). saved_routes rows are owner-scoped; the hourly /api/public/jobs/route-alerts job re-checks them, emails at most once per 12 h per route and only moves the stored baseline when it alerts, so slow deterioration keeps accumulating.
- waypoint-edit.ts owns the tested 500 ms map gestures and immutable point/track updates; inserted controls snap to the actual track in traversal order, preserving channel vertices and rejecting land-crossing edits. Tratta persists the edited track with its controls and keys forecasts by their new coordinates so inserts and moves recalculate the route.

- ais.ts: pure tested AIS decoding (NMEA 1/2/3/18, Signal K) and CPA/TCPA risk (danger <1 NM in 30 min, caution <2 NM in 60 min); web targets via authenticated fetchAisTargets (aisstream, 15 s sample; login required to protect the free aisstream key quota), onboard via browser WebSocket so it must be wss on https.
- onboard-alarms.ts: pure tested anchor-drag (metres > radius) and wind-shift (wrapped angle > tolerance) rules; /allarmi uses browser GPS watch, Web Audio siren and Screen Wake Lock, client-only, no storage.
